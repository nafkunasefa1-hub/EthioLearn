D/ it charges the crystal positively
Correct Answer: A) it increases the number of positive holes into the silicon lattice.

positive charge carrier. This is why boron is called an acceptor impurity, as it "accepts"
electrons, creating holes in the silicon lattice.
9. What is the force that causes centripetal acceleration when a car goes around a curve?
a) Friction b) Gravity c) Normal d) Tension
A) Friction
Explanation: When a car goes around a curve, the friction between the tires and the road
provides the necessary centripetal force that keeps the car moving in a circular path. This
frictional force acts toward the center of the curve, causing the centripetal acceleration
needed for circular motion. Without sufficient friction, the car would not be able to
follow the curve and would instead move in a straight line due to inertia.

around the Earth. The gravitational force between the Earth and the moon pulls the moon towards the
Earth, but the moon also has a tangential velocity that keeps it moving forward. This combination of the
moon's velocity and the gravitational pull creates a curved path, resulting in the moon orbiting the Earth
rather than falling straight down.
